This assignment aims to critically examine the relationship between human and automatic evaluation in NLP. Your objective is to assess how well automatic metrics align with human judgment and to identify any significant patterns or discrepancies.

## Steps and Guidelines:

1. Task Selection:
    - Choose a single NLP task (e.g., machine translation, text summarization, sentiment analysis etc.).

2. **Model Outputs Collection:**
    - Gather system outputs from three to four different models performing the selected task.

3. **Human Evaluation:**
    - Conduct a manual evaluation using a minimum of five human evaluators.
    - Define clear evaluation criteria (specify potential criteria such as fluency, adequacy, coherence, or task-specific measures) to ensure consistency in human judgment.

4. **Automatic Evaluation:**
    - Assess the same model outputs using widely used automatic evaluation metrics relevant to the chosen task.

5. **Analysis and Correlation:**
    - Compare human scores with automatic metric scores.
    - Identify patterns, correlations, and notable discrepancies between human and automated evaluations.
